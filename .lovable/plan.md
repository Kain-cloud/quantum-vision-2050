

## "Jeta në vitin 2050" — Interactive Educational Web App

A visually stunning, scroll-driven interactive presentation about quantum physics and innovations in 2050, fully in Albanian, targeting ages 10–16.

### Design System
- **Colors**: Deep space blue (`#0a0e27`), electric purple (`#7c3aed`), neon green (`#22d3ee`), gold (`#fbbf24`), white
- **Style**: Futuristic with holographic gradients, glowing borders, particle effects, and smooth scroll animations
- **Typography**: Bold headings with gradient text, clean readable body text
- **Animations**: Fade-in on scroll, floating particles, pulsing glows, parallax backgrounds

### Pages & Sections (Single-Page Scroll Experience)

1. **Hero / Introduction** — Full-screen Earth-from-space scene with animated title "Mirë se vini në vitin 2050", floating particles, and scroll indicator

2. **Çfarë është fizika kuantike?** — Interactive cards explaining:
   - Superposition (animated particle in multiple states)
   - Quantum tunneling (particle teleportation visual)
   - Observer effect (interactive "observe" button that changes particle behavior)
   - Simple metaphors with icons and short Albanian text

3. **Inovacionet kryesore në vitin 2050** — Six feature cards with hover effects:
   - Kompjuterët kuantikë (quantum computers)
   - Siguria e internetit (quantum cryptography)
   - Energji e pastër (clean energy)
   - Transport i avancuar (advanced transport)
   - Inteligjencë artificiale (AI & robots)
   - Mjekësi moderne (modern medicine)

4. **Si do të ndryshojë jeta e përditshme?** — Illustrated smart home scene with interactive hotspots (voice-controlled home, VR learning, Mars video calls)

5. **Hapësira** — Space exploration section with exact Albanian text provided, animated star field, facts displayed as floating cards, Mars base illustration

6. **Conclusion** — Inspiring closing message with call-to-wonder, animated timeline showing "E ardhmja po ndërtohet sot"

### Interactive Elements
- Scroll-triggered animations (elements fade/slide in as you scroll)
- Expandable "Mëso më shumë" (Learn more) accordions on each topic
- Animated particle backgrounds throughout
- Hover effects on all cards with glow and scale
- Smooth section navigation dots on the side

### Technical Approach
- Single-page React app with smooth scrolling sections
- CSS animations and Intersection Observer for scroll effects
- SVG illustrations and CSS art for futuristic visuals
- Fully responsive for tablet/mobile viewing
- All text in Albanian as specified
